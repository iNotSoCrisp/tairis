# Ideation Document - Tairis

## Problem Statement
In emergencies, people often don’t know what to do before a doctor arrives.  
Most first-aid information online is confusing or takes too long to find.

---

## Proposed Solution
Tairis provides quick and clear first-aid steps using a decision tree.  
The user just answers a few yes/no questions and gets the right steps to follow instantly.

---

## Tech Stack
- Frontend: React  
- Backend: Express.js  
- Database: Postgres-Supabase  
- Runtime: Node.js
